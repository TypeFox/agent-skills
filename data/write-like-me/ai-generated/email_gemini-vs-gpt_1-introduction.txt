To give you a little more context ahead of our call: Vanguard Modulytics specializes in modular sensory arrays and motor control units for automated logistics environments. Currently, our customers configure these systems using deeply nested, highly complex JSON files. It is incredibly error-prone because our hardware has strict physical and electrical dependency rules. For example, if a specific optical sensor is set to a high polling frequency, certain power-saving modes on the paired control board become strictly unavailable. We currently catch these logic errors way too late in the deployment cycle, causing frustration and delays.

Our vision is to replace this clunky process with a clean, human-readable DSL embedded in a lightweight web editor. We are particularly interested in the AI angle to help bridge the knowledge gap—ideally, an assistant could suggest valid configuration blocks on the fly and explain in plain English why certain hardware combinations violate our constraints.
