## Bridging the Divide: Enabling Real-Time Collaboration Across Non-TypeScript IDEs

As development environments become increasingly fragmented, real-time collaboration shouldn't be limited by your choice of IDE. In this session, we unveil the OCT Service Process—a standalone JSON-RPC bridge designed to bring robust, real-time collaborative coding to non-TypeScript ecosystems. We will walk through a live IntelliJ integration, demonstrating how to break down silos between tools and empower developer teams to collaborate frictionlessly, no matter their preferred tech stack. Attendees will leave with a practical understanding of how to implement cross-IDE syncing in their own environments.

## Architecting the Next Generation of Domain-Specific Web Applications with Baukasten

Building modern, web-based domain-specific applications (DSAs) often forces developers into a frustrating tradeoff between deep IDE integration and flexible, standalone UIs. Enter Baukasten: a cutting-edge UI toolkit built to eliminate this compromise. This talk explores how Baukasten empowers teams to rapidly engineer rich, domain-specific tooling that works seamlessly across web platforms and modern IDEs. We will dive into architectural best practices and showcase live examples, equipping you with the tools to drastically streamline your DSA development lifecycle.

## Need for Speed: Architecting High-Performance Language Engineering Tools

Speed is the ultimate feature in language engineering. In this deep dive, we dissect the architecture behind Fastbelt—our newly developed framework built explicitly for maximum performance. We will explore the technical bottlenecks that have historically plagued domain-specific languages (DSLs) and demonstrate the architectural innovations required to overcome them. Whether you are building complex compiler toolchains, intelligent editors, or lightweight parsers, this session provides actionable insights into achieving unprecedented performance in your language tooling.

## The Multi-Player AI Developer: Integrating Autonomous Agents into Collaborative Coding

AI coding assistants are powerful, but they operate almost exclusively in single-player mode. What happens when we invite AI into a real-time collaborative session? In this talk, we introduce the OCT Agent, a breakthrough integration that connects ACP-compatible coding agents directly into live collaborative environments. We'll demonstrate how multiple developers can simultaneously prompt the AI, review diffs as a team, and accept changes synchronously. Discover the future of pair programming—where your next pair is an AI, and your whole team is in the driver's seat.

Generic AI coding agents struggle with highly specialized, proprietary domains. The solution? Equipping them with Domain-Specific Languages (DSLs). We will showcase how to leverage the Langium AI CLI to rapidly bootstrap DSL-aware agents, measure their accuracy through improved eval integrations, and deploy AI that actually understands your enterprise's unique domain constraints.

Everyone is building with AI, but very few are building reliable, domain-specific AI systems that enterprise architectures can actually trust.
